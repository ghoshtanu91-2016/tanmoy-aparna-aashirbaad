On the 18th of June 2026, under the blessings of the elders and the watchful eyes of the stars, Tanmoy and Aparna took their first formal steps toward a shared life. The sacred traditions ahead

সামনের পবিত্র আচার-অনুষ্ঠান

Paka Katha & Aashirbaad — পাকা কথা ও আশীর্বাদ ✦ Completed · 18 June 2026

আজকের অনুষ্ঠান

The formal confirmation of the match and the blessing ceremony. The groom's family visits with gifts — mishti, fish, new sarees — and both families exchange words of commitment. Registry Marriage application submitted on this day.

Aiburobhaat — আইবুড়োভাত

বিবাহের আগের ভোজ

The last meal bride and groom share as unmarried individuals, lovingly cooked and served by their own families. A bittersweet farewell to their previous lives, celebrated with joy.

Gaye Holud — গায়ে হলুদ

হলুদের অনুষ্ঠান

Turmeric paste mixed with mustard oil and herbs is applied by family members. The golden hue of haldi blesses the couple with radiance and wards off evil before the wedding day.

Shubho Drishti — শুভদৃষ্টি

প্রথম শুভ দর্শন

The auspicious first look — the bride is carried on a wooden seat by her brothers. She and the groom gaze into each other's eyes through seven betel leaves, the moment filling the air with ululation.

Mala Badal — মালাবদল

ফুলের মালা বিনিময়

The exchange of flower garlands three times, symbolizing mutual acceptance. The bride is lifted three times by the groom's sisters amid laughter and the fragrance of tuberose.

Sindoor Daan — সিঁদুরদান

সিঁদুর পরানো

The most sacred moment — the groom applies vermilion to the bride's parting, completing the rites and marking the beginning of their life as husband and wife.
