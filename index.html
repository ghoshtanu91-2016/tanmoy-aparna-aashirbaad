Bengali Wedding Rituals

The sacred traditions

পবিত্র আচার-অনুষ্ঠান

Aashirbaad — আশীর্বাদ

আশীর্বাদ

The engagement ceremony where elders bless the couple. The groom's family visits the bride's home with gifts — sweets, fish, and new clothes — and both families exchange blessings for the forthcoming union.

Aiburobhaat — আইবুড়োভাত

বিবাহের আগের ভোজ

The last meal the bride and groom share as unmarried individuals, prepared and served by their own families. A bittersweet farewell to their previous lives, celebrated with joy and a grand feast.

Gaye Holud — গায়ে হলুদ

হলুদের অনুষ্ঠান

Turmeric paste mixed with mustard oil and fragrant herbs is applied to the bride and groom by family members. The golden hue of haldi is believed to bless the couple with a radiant complexion and ward off evil.

Shubho Drishti — শুভদৃষ্টি

প্রথম দর্শন

The auspicious first look — the bride and groom gaze into each other's eyes as the bride is carried on a wooden seat by her brothers. Seven betel leaves held before the eyes add a touch of delicate ritual magic.

Mala Badal — মালাবদল

ফুলের মালা বিনিময়

The exchange of garlands, symbolizing mutual acceptance. The bride and groom exchange flower garlands three times while sisters of the groom lift the bride three times — filling the air with laughter and ululation.

Sindoor Daan — সিঁদুরদান

সিঁদুর পরানো

The most sacred moment: the groom applies vermilion to the bride's parting. This act formally marks her as a married woman in the Bengali Hindu tradition, completing the sacred rites of the wedding.
